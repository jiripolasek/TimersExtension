# Privacy Policy

*Last updated November 27, 2026*

This policy covers **Timers for Command Palette**, a Windows extension for Microsoft PowerToys Command Palette published by **Jiří Polášek**, and its website.

## App data and use

Timers saves timer and stopwatch names, durations, laps, templates, and preferences locally on your Windows device. It uses that data to run timers and stopwatches, deliver alerts, and restore your saved items and settings. If you enter personal information in a timer or stopwatch name, it stays on your device with that item.

Timers does not require an account or send your app data to the publisher or to third parties. The extension includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

## Your data controls

You can view and delete saved items from the **Timers** and **Stopwatches** pages in Command Palette. You can change your preferences in the extension settings.

Use Windows Settings to uninstall Timers. Where Windows offers **Reset** for the app, you can use it to clear the data Windows manages for the installed package.

## Microsoft services

Windows and the Microsoft Store may independently process information, such as diagnostic or Store activity data. See the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement).

## Website

This website saves your theme preference in your browser. You can remove it by clearing site data in your browser settings. The site uses no analytics scripts.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. The [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) describes how GitHub handles visitor data.

## Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page and revise the date above if the app’s features or data practices change.
