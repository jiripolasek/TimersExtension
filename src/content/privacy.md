## Windows extension

Timers saves timer and stopwatch names, durations, recorded laps, and templates on your PC so they are available when you reopen Command Palette. Those files are stored under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PowerToys\CommandPalette\Timers`. Extension preferences are saved in the Command Palette settings file. Timer alerts and sounds run locally.

You can remove individual entries from Timers or Stopwatches. After closing Command Palette, you can delete the local Timers folder to remove its saved timers, templates, and stopwatches.

## Website

This website stores your light or dark theme preference in your browser. You can remove it by clearing site data in your browser settings. The site loads its fonts and images from the same host and has no analytics scripts.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for how GitHub handles visitor data. Microsoft Store and other linked sites have their own privacy policies.
